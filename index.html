Why clean?

The air you breathe at home has a direct effect on your health.

Indoor air pollution from mold, mildew and dust mites can trigger breathing problems. Cleanco removes the dirt, dust and airborne pollutants that build up in your furnace and ducts, so the air moving through your home or business is cleaner.

How we clean

The 2 Step HI-TECH™ duct cleaning procedure.

✓Conforms to National Air Duct Cleaning Standards (NADCA)
Step 01

Brush & loosen

Rotary brush systems and power whips driven by powerful air compressors work through your ducts, breaking up caked-on dirt and odour.

  • Rotary brushes
  • Power whips
  • Air compressors
Step 02

Vacuum & remove

Our custom-built, high-powered vacuum trucks pull the loosened dirt, dust and airborne pollutants out of your furnace and duct system.

  • Truck-mounted vacuum
  • Furnace & ducts
  • Homes & businesses

Custom-built vacuum trucks, not portable machines.

Every Cleanco job is done with our own high-powered truck units, powerful air compressors, rotary brushes and power whips.

✕We do not use portable electric vacuums or converted carpet-cleaning attachments.
